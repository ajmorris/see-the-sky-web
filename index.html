Questions people ask

How does it know I'm outside without a timer?

Your Apple Watch has an ambient light sensor and already records "Time in Daylight" to Apple Health. See the Sky reads that, then cross-checks it with your movement, your workouts, and the places you go every day. A few times a day it looks back and turns those signals into outdoor sessions.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Apple Watch Series 6 or later, Apple Watch SE (2nd generation) or later, or any Apple Watch Ultra, running watchOS 10 or newer. That's every model from the last several years. Your iPhone needs iOS 17 or later.

Does it count sitting by a window?

The daylight sensor alone would. See the Sky won't. It learns your home, office, and other regular indoor spots, and it treats daylight there with suspicion unless your movement says you actually went out. And if it gets one wrong, one swipe fixes it and teaches it.

Is it real time?

No, and that's deliberate. See the Sky is a daily metric, like a step count, not a stopwatch. Your total updates a few times a day and the widget shows an "as of" time. That's what lets it run all day with almost no battery cost.

What about the battery?

See the Sky never runs GPS continuously. It uses data your watch is already collecting, so the target is under three percent extra battery a day on both watch and phone.

My kids don't have watches. How do their hours work?

Create a profile for each of them. When See the Sky detects a session on your watch, tap the faces of whoever was with you. You can also set a default ("the kids are usually with me") so it's pre-filled. Manual entry is there for the days grandma takes them to the beach.

Do I need an account?

No. Solo use needs nothing. Family sync between two parents uses iCloud, so it's your existing Apple ID and nothing else. There's no login, no password, no server of ours holding your data.

Where does my location data go?

Nowhere. The places See the Sky learns stay on your device and are never synced, even to your other devices. Family sync shares session times and profile names only.
